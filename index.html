Our Firm

A Switzerland & Austria based trading firm, built for access.

The Swiss-Austrian Venture Organization (SVO) simplifies multiple-business ownership for investors. We manage a diversified portfolio of operating businesses and offer leveraged investment opportunities with disciplined, built-in risk management.

Our mission is to make sophisticated strategies — previously available only to institutional investors — accessible to qualified individuals, with transparency and security at the core.

For the first time, SVO welcomes investors with a net worth under $1M and starting capital under $100 — democratising access to serious investment opportunities.
